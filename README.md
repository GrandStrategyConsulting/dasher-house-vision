Project Objective

Build a world-class, elegant, responsive website for Dasher House Enterprises, a boutique real estate development, hospitality, property portfolio, consultancy, and partnership platform founded by Lisa Moore.

This website is separate from Lisa Moore’s broader personal or brand website. It should focus specifically on the real estate portfolio, Dasher House, event and short-stay hospitality, development projects, consultancy services, and partnership opportunities.

The site must position Dasher House Enterprises as a serious, founder-led real estate development and property portfolio company, not simply an Airbnb business or event venue.

Core Positioning

Dasher House Enterprises is a boutique real estate development and property portfolio company focused on hospitality-led assets, community living, thoughtful development, property activation, and long-term value creation.

The company currently includes Dasher House, the flagship property used for events, curated gatherings, retreats, private experiences, and Airbnb/short-stay hospitality.

Dasher House Enterprises is also developing a broader real estate pipeline, including multi-unit homes, community living concepts, and opportunities in areas experiencing regeneration, revitalization, and long-term growth potential.

The website should communicate:

Dasher House Enterprises develops, activates, and stewards properties with purpose, character, and long-term value.

1. Guests and Event Clients

People who want to book Dasher House for Airbnb stays, private events, retreats, workshops, curated gatherings, or intimate celebrations.

2. Property Owners and Aspiring Developers

People seeking consultancy support around property strategy, Airbnb readiness, event property activation, development feasibility, or community living concepts.

3. Developers, Investors, and JV Partners

People interested in partnering with Dasher House Enterprises on high-growth, regeneration-area, multifamily, hospitality-led, or community-centered real estate opportunities.

4. Community Stakeholders and Lenders

People who need to understand the company’s credibility, development philosophy, community focus, and long-term portfolio direction.
